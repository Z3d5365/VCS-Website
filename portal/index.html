Images & photos

Upload straight from your phone or computer, or paste an image URL — logo, food, rooms, staff, signage, menu shots.

Send us normal pictures. Everyday phone snaps are perfect — you don't need a photographer, an editing app or a particular size. We enhance them for you: brighten and colour-correct, straighten and crop, remove the clutter, cut out backgrounds where it helps, and compress them so the site still loads in under two seconds. Slightly dark, slightly crooked or a bit cluttered is fine — send it anyway and we'll fix it. Screenshots, WhatsApp forwards and photos of a printed menu all work.
