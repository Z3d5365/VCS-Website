Why local search wins customers

Most people looking for a restaurant, resort or local service don't scroll past the map. They tap one of the top few results, check the photos and reviews, and go. If your business isn't there — or the listing is half-empty with the wrong hours — that customer goes to a competitor who showed up properly.

Local SEO fixes that from both sides: a complete, active Google Business Profile, and a fast website with the right structured data behind it. In competitive local markets, being easy to find is often worth more than any advert.
