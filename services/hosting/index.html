Why upkeep matters

A website isn't a "set it and forget it" thing. Certificates expire, software needs patching, and a site that goes down during a busy weekend costs you real bookings. Monthly upkeep means someone is watching — updates get applied, backups run every night, and if something breaks it's fixed before most people notice.

It also means the small changes actually happen. New menu prices, a public holiday's opening hours, a fresh photo — send them over and they go live, instead of sitting on a to-do list for months.
